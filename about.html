Hi! I am Ally, short for Alethea, a 4th-year Biopsychology major and Applied Psychology minor at UCSB. I am originally from San Diego and spend most of my free time outdoors - surfing, snorkeling, or doing pretty much any sport I can find. I am also in leadership for Epic Movement, a campus club, and I work as a research assistant at the Koegel Autism Center on campus.

My interest in autism is both personal and professional. My brother has autism, and growing up alongside him gave me an early and ongoing education in what it really means to navigate the world as an autistic person - the strengths, the challenges, and the very real ways that other people's assumptions can get in the way. That experience is a big part of why I am pursuing occupational therapy post-graduation, with a focus on working with neurodivergent kids and young adults.

The projects on this site were created for WRIT 105M: Multimedia Composition at UCSB. Each one uses a different medium to challenge misconceptions about autism in media and push back against narrow, often harmful portrayals that shape how autistic individuals are perceived and treated in schools, workplaces, and everyday life.

Guiding perspective

Representation should expand how we see people.

This site avoids puzzle-piece imagery and clinical stereotypes. Instead, it uses layered visuals, warm colors, and reflective writing to center complexity, dignity, and lived experience.
