Why this topic matters

Autism is more than a character trope.

Growing up, the autism I saw in media looked nothing like the autism I knew at home. The portrayals were always the same: the quiet genius, the emotionless loner, the character whose entire identity was defined by their diagnosis. But my brother - and every autistic person I have come to know through my work at the Koegel Autism Center - is so much more than any of those images.

This portfolio is a collection of three projects created for WRIT 105M that examine how autism is misrepresented in media and society, and what those misrepresentations actually cost. Each project approaches that argument through a different lens: a layered photo essay, an interactive digital exploration, and a satirical Instagram carousel.

My hope is that you leave this site questioning the lens through which autism is commonly viewed - and more aware of how media shapes the assumptions we make about others.
